Breath Trainer

How it works

Pick a preset, tap Start, then put the phone down or lock it: the tones keep playing. With the classic beeps a long low tone means hold, two rising notes mean breathe and three ticks count down the last seconds; Hear the cues plays each sound. On iPhone use a Safari tab, not a Home Screen app, and do not mute the tab.

This is running as a Home Screen app, where iOS stops the cues when the screen locks. For reliable audio open the page in a Safari tab instead.

Cue guide

Background audio on iPhone

The whole session is rendered to one audio track when you tap Start, so the cues keep playing when the screen is locked or you switch apps. Use the page in a Safari tab rather than as a Home Screen app, do not mute the tab, and leave the session playing. Lock-screen play and pause controls work. Cues play even when the ringer switch is on silent. Starting a session pauses music from other apps; turn on Mix with other apps to keep the music playing instead.

Settings, presets and history are stored only in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Backup

Settings, presets and history live only in this browser. Export a file to move them to another device or keep them safe; importing replaces settings and presets and merges the history.
